I graduated from Indiana University with computer science, math, and Spanish degrees in 2017 and completed my PhD in computer science at UC San Diego in 2024. I am now an assistant teaching professor in the computer science department at Purdue University. Courses I have taught include CS 182 (Foundations of Computer Science), CS 243 (Artificial Intelligence Basics), and CS 441 (Data Science Capstone). Broadly speaking, my research interests include privacy, human-computer interaction, and education.
